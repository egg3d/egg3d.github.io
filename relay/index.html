Local desktop publishing tool

Relay

Prepare and publish original game-development media from your own computer.

Relay by egg 3d Studio helps its operator review a video or image, approve the exact account and post details, send through a supported official platform integration, and keep local publication evidence. It is currently a locally operated desktop tool. This page does not offer public sign-up or a hosted publishing service.

What Relay does

PrepareImport and review game-development videos and images.
PublishUse supported official integrations after account connection and per-post approval.
VerifyCheck available platform status and keep local evidence of the result.

Current integration status

YouTube private publishing has been tested through its official API. TikTok integration is being tested in TikTok's Sandbox with private account and SELF_ONLY safety conditions. Other platform integrations are not represented here as publicly available services.
