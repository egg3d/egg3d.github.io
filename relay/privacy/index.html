Privacy Policy

Updated September 26, 2026. This policy describes Relay, the locally operated desktop publishing tool by egg 3d Studio.

Data processed

Relay may process connected platform account identifiers, OAuth access and refresh credentials, media files selected by the operator, titles, captions and other post metadata, remote post identifiers and status, local publication evidence, and technical diagnostics. Relay does not collect health or payment information for its publishing workflow.

Storage and use

Relay runs primarily on the operator's PC. It copies imported media into its local managed storage and keeps post history, account bindings, and evidence in local files and a local database. OAuth credentials are held in Relay's Windows user-protected credential storage, separate from ordinary publication records. Raw credentials are not sent to this public website. Relay uses this information to prepare a post, confirm the selected account and media, send an approved post, and check the result available from the platform.

Platform transfers

When an authorized post is sent, Relay transfers the selected content and metadata to the chosen platform through its official integration. Connected services may include YouTube/Google and TikTok; future integrations will be identified before use. Those services handle received data under their own terms and privacy policies. Relay does not sell user data. This website does not receive OAuth credentials, selected media, or local publication history.

Retention and deletion

Local media copies, post records, diagnostics, and evidence remain on the operator's PC until the operator removes the local Relay data. Removing local files does not remove a post or data already held by a connected platform. The operator may revoke platform authorization in the platform's account settings and remove Relay's local credentials. No automatic deletion of remote posts is performed.

Security and limits

Relay limits its local server to loopback access and uses per-post authorization and a live send gate. No system can guarantee that a platform API, network, or local device will always be available or secure. A result that cannot be verified is kept as uncertain rather than automatically reposted.
